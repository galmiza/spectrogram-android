Privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This privacy policy governs your use of the software application Spectrogram (“Application”) for mobile devices that was created by Galmiza ("Developer"). The Application renders in real-time the spectrogram of the sound recorded by the device microphone. It allows basic sound processing and spectrogram rendering customization which may be configured from the Application.

What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

User Provided Information

The Application stores your preferences such as recording and rendering properties.

Microphone

The Application asks for permission to use the microphone, only to compute and display the spectrogram while it is on screen. The sound is analysed on the device and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

Automatically Collected Information

The Application does not collect any information about you, your device, your connection settings.
The audio samples recorded to compute the spectrogram are not stored in the device or any other storage service.

Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

The Application does not collect any information about the location of your mobile device.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

No third party may see or have access to the information you provided in the Application.

What are my opt-out rights?

You can remove the information you provided by uninstalling the Application.

Data Retention Policy, Managing Your Information

The User Provided information is stored in the device as long as the Application is installed.

Children

Not applicable.

Security

The User Provided information is only stored in the device. Thus it is only visible by you. Please be aware that no system can prevent all potential security breaches.

Your Consent

By using the Application, you are consenting to our processing of your information as set forth in this Privacy Policy now and as amended by us. "Processing,” means using your preferences to compute and render the spectrogram (of the sound recorded by the microphone).

Contact us

If you have any questions regarding privacy while using the Application, or have questions about our practices, please contact us via email at galmiza@gmail.com.
